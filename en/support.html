FAQ

A country isn't colored.
Photos need location data. Photos taken with location turned off, or with location removed, can't be placed. Also check that FootPrints has access under Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

I visited a country but have no photos.
Tap the country on the map and turn on "I've been here" in the detail sheet. It will appear hatched on the map.

How do I mark places I want to go?
Tap a country you haven't visited and turn on "Want to go". It appears with a dashed outline on the map and is listed in Statistics.

A photo is counted in a country I never visited.
Photos others sent you, for example, may carry a location that isn't where you were. Touch and hold the photo in the detail sheet and choose "Not Taken Here" to leave it out of your totals. You can put it back from the left-out photos in the same sheet.

Photos near a border or coast are placed in the neighboring country.
Locations are matched against map data on your device and may be off near borders. Leave out the wrong photos as described above and mark the right country with "I've been here".

Can I see where I took my photos?
Tap the layers button at the top right of the map and choose Photo Locations to show the places where you took photos as small red dots. Photos taken close together share one dot, and places with more photos get bigger dots. Tap a dot to see the photos taken there; Trips Here at the top opens the trips those photos belong to. The dots are never included in map images you share or in what you share with family and friends.

How do I share my map?
Tap the share button at the bottom left to create an image of your map with the number of countries and a legend. You can choose the whole world or your home country, and a light or dark look.

How are trips created?
The place where you took photos on the most days is treated as home. Photos taken away from home are grouped into trips by date. Tap the suitcase button at the bottom left to see your trips, their routes, and a calendar. To keep a photo out of a trip, use "Not Taken Here" as described above.

A visit to my family home and an outing from there became one trip.
In the trip's details, tap "Add as a Regular Place" to register the place. Photos taken within 2 km of it are grouped as visits there, and outings from there become separate trips. You can remove regular places under "Regular Places" in settings (the gear button).

How do I compare maps with family or friends?
Tap "Friends & Family" at the bottom, choose a group such as “Family” or “Friends”, then tap "Invite" to send an invitation by Messages or Mail. When they open the link, you can overlay your maps and see the countries you've both visited and want to visit. You choose what each group can see (countries, regions, places to go, trip destinations and months). Photos and notes are never shared, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Are videos included?
Yes. Like photos, the location and date of your videos color the map and are included in trips and the calendar. Tap a video in trip or place details to play it.

Can I use FootPrints with Siri?
Yes. Ask “How many countries have I visited in FootPrints”, “My travels this year in FootPrints” or “On this day in FootPrints”. You can also say “Open my year in review in FootPrints” or “Show my trips in FootPrints”. The same actions are available in the Shortcuts app.

How do I add a widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose FootPrints. There are three widgets: Countries Visited, This Year’s Travels, and On This Day. Widgets update when you open FootPrints.

I opened the invitation link, but the map wasn't added.
The other person needs FootPrints installed and must be signed in to iCloud. Invitation links can't be opened in an in-app browser. If the link arrived in an app other than Messages, touch and hold it and open it in Safari. If you installed FootPrints after opening the link, open the link again.

How do I send trip photos to the people I traveled with?
In a trip's details, tap "Select", choose photos, then tap "Share". You can remove where the photos were taken before sending.

Can I keep a trip on one page?
In a trip, tap the book button (Trip Album) at the top right. It puts the title, dates, route map, places, your notes and nine photos on one page. Photos are chosen automatically; tap Choose Photos below the album to pick your own. Use the share button at the top right to send or save it as an image or a PDF.

What badges are there?
Besides the number of countries, continents and regions of your home country, there are badges for countries in one year, countries in one trip, years in a row with a trip, and Japan's eight regions (when your region is Japan). Statistics shows when you earned each badge and how many more you need.

Can I add notes to trips and days?
Add a title and notes in a trip's details. To add a note for a day, tap the pencil next to that day in the trip, or open the day in the calendar. While viewing a photo full screen, tap Add a caption to give that photo a caption; captions also appear in the trip album. Trips with a title show the destination below the title in the list.

Can I zoom more precisely?
Touch and hold the zoom indicator at the bottom right, then move your finger up or down to zoom around the center of the view. A quick tap shows the whole map again.

Can I see trips next to the map on iPad or Mac?
On wide screens, trips, details and statistics open on the right side of the map. Tap a country on the map while the panel is open to switch to it.

Are regions shown for other countries too?
Yes. For any country you've visited, with photos or "I've been here", zooming in switches to its states, provinces, or prefectures. From a region's details you can open the whole country, and from a country's details you can open the regions you've visited.

How do I change the country used at the top and in Statistics?
Tap the gear button at the bottom left and choose a country under "Country for regions". By default, the country in your device's region settings is used.

How do I change the language?
Go to Settings > Apps > FootPrints > Language. FootPrints is available in English, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. Tapping "Language" in the app's settings opens the same page.

Only some of my photos are scanned.
If you allowed access to selected photos only, only those photos are scanned. Touch and hold the refresh button at the bottom left to change the selection.

Where is my data stored?
Photo scan results stay on your device. Some data, such as places you added by hand, syncs between your devices through your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for details.
