FAQ

A country isn't colored.
Photos need location data. Photos taken with location turned off, or with location removed, can't be placed. Also check that FootPrints has access under Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

I visited a country but have no photos.
Tap the country on the map and turn on "I've been here" in the detail sheet. It will appear hatched on the map.

How do I mark places I want to go?
Tap a country you haven't visited and turn on "Want to go". It appears with a dashed outline on the map and is listed in Statistics.

A photo is counted in a country I never visited.
Photos others sent you, for example, may carry a location that isn't where you were. Touch and hold the photo in the detail sheet and choose "Not Taken Here" to leave it out of your totals. You can put it back from the left-out photos in the same sheet.

Photos near a border or coast are placed in the neighboring country.
Locations are matched against map data on your device and may be off near borders. Leave out the wrong photos as described above and mark the right country with "I've been here".

How do I share my map?
Tap the share button at the bottom left to create an image of your map with the number of countries and a legend. You can choose the whole world or your home country, and a light or dark look.

How are trips created?
The place where you took photos on the most days is treated as home. Photos taken away from home are grouped into trips by date. Tap the suitcase button at the bottom left to see your trips, their routes, and a calendar. To keep a photo out of a trip, use "Not Taken Here" as described above.

Are regions shown for other countries too?
Yes. For any country you've visited, with photos or "I've been here", zooming in switches to its states, provinces, or prefectures. From a region's details you can open the whole country, and from a country's details you can open the regions you've visited.

How do I change the country used at the top and in Statistics?
Tap the gear button at the bottom left and choose a country under "Country for regions". By default, the country in your device's region settings is used.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the FootPrints icon on the Home Screen and choose a widget size, or add it from "Edit Home Screen". The widget updates when you open FootPrints.

How do I change the language?
Go to Settings > Apps > FootPrints > Language to switch between English and Japanese. Tapping "Language" in the app's settings opens the same page.

Only some of my photos are scanned.
If you allowed access to selected photos only, only those photos are scanned. Touch and hold the refresh button at the bottom left to change the selection.

Where is my data stored?
Photo scan results stay on your device. Some data, such as places you added by hand, syncs between your devices through your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for details.
